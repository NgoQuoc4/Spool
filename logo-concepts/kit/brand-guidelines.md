# Spool Brand Guidelines & Logo Kit

## 1. Brand Concept: The Spool Vortex
**Spool** represents the modern hub of conversation — an origin point where threads of ideas, opinions, and stories wind and unwind in continuous motion. 

The **Spool Vortex** mark distills this narrative into pure geometry:
- **Central Spindle:** The focal core / community hub.
- **Twin Helical Thread Arms:** 180° rotational symmetry embodying broadcasting, circulating, reposting, and ongoing dialogue.
- **Topological Fluidity:** Seamless, clean curves that remain instantly recognizable at 16px as well as 512px.

---

## 3. Clear Space & Minimum Sizing
- **Clear Space:** Maintain an exclusion zone equal to the diameter of the central core (`X = 40px` relative to 256px canvas) around all sides of the mark.
- **Minimum Symbol Size:** `16 × 16 px` (standard favicon size).
- **Minimum Lockup Width:** `96 px` for horizontal combination mark.

## 5. Misuse Guidelines
- ❌ Do **not** stretch, skew, or distort the proportions of the mark.
- ❌ Do **not** apply heavy drop-shadows, emboss filters, or wood textures.
- ❌ Do **not** rotate the mark off its true 180° symmetrical axis.
- ❌ Do **not** place the black mark on dark backgrounds without sufficient contrast.
